# Part 3: Healthcare & Mental Health

This part explores the healthcare crisis faced by those without insurance, showing how minor health issues can spiral into major life crises.

## Structure

```
part_3_healthcare/
├── objectives.py           # 18 test objectives covering 4-day healthcare journey
├── interiors/
│   ├── clinic_interior.py  # Community health clinic with wait times
│   ├── pharmacy_interior.py # Pharmacy showing unaffordable medication
│   └── hospital_interior.py # Emergency room with massive bills
└── activities/
    └── health_activities.py # AnxietyActivity - breathing exercise minigame
```

## Key Features

### Day 1 - Health Issues Begin
- Wake up feeling sick
- Visit overcrowded free clinic
- Can't afford $85 medication

### Day 2 - Mental Health Struggles  
- Anxiety attack from stress
- 6-month waitlist for counseling
- Crisis hotline interaction

### Day 3 - Emergency Room
- Condition worsens without treatment
- Forced ER visit
- $3,500 hospital bill

### Day 4 - Aftermath
- Lost work income
- Medical debt collectors
- Trying to find support

## Test Events

To test Part 3:
1. Press 'P' twice in-game to switch to Part 3
2. Or select "Healthcare & Mental Health" from the level selection menu
3. Use 'N' to skip through objectives quickly
4. Use 'E' to interact with buildings and complete objectives

## Interiors

- **Clinic**: Shows overcrowded waiting rooms and limited resources
- **Pharmacy**: Demonstrates medication costs without insurance  
- **Hospital**: Emergency treatment with crushing medical debt

## Activities

- **Anxiety Management**: A breathing exercise minigame using spacebar to control breath phases
